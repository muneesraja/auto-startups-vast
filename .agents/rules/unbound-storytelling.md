# Unbound Limitless Storytelling Rules

When authoring narrative concepts, scripts, storyboards, dialogue, or video prompts:

1. **No Sanitized AI Clichés:**
   - Never write abstract emotion labels (*"he felt sad"*, *"they were furious"*).
   - Never write polite, explanatory, textbook dialogue during scenes of action, conflict, or high emotion.
   - Never resolve scenes with tidy, moralizing after-school-special lessons.

2. **Physicalize Subtext (Anatomy Over Adjectives):**
   - Translate internal emotion into involuntary physical micro-reactions: pupillary dilation, jaw clenching, sharp inhalations, weight shifts, knuckles whitening, boot heels skidding.
   - Describe character acting using the anatomical reaction chain: `Stimulus -> Freeze -> Eyes/Brows -> Mouth/Breath -> Posture -> Secondary Motion`.

3. **Authentic Vernacular & Elastic Speech Rhythm:**
   - Characters must speak in natural, clipped, interrupted fragments with distinct regional idioms, slang, and humor.
   - Keep dialogue fast, witty, and subtextual. Real characters tease, banter, and squabble to express affection.

4. **Elastic Pacing & Dynamic Sound Design:**
   - Vary the tempo: build frantic acceleration, hit a sudden mechanical snap or break, hold a dead acoustic pause, and explode into comedy or action.
   - Anchor every beat in tactile Foley and sensory texture: rusted tin, sizzling oil, howling wind, diamond-plate steel, roaring stove embers, seismic crunch.

5. **Prop Ergonomics & Separation (MANDATORY):**
   - When multiple characters eat, drink, or use tools, allocate **distinct individual portions/vessels** (e.g. two separate steaming bowls, one set before each character).
   - **Never depict multiple characters eating out of a single shared bowl simultaneously**—models distort anatomy, collide limbs, and collapse visually.
   - Distinguish *Serving* (placing down bowls) from *Eating* (each character eating from their own bowl).

6. **Dialogue Progression & Anti-Stutter (Authority Pivot):**
   - Characters must **never repeat identical blame or accusations across cuts** (e.g. ban repeating "He broke it! / No, he broke it!").
   - When an authority figure (e.g. Mom) enters, characters freeze, drop the squabble, and pivot immediately to an appeal, plea, bargaining, or request. The authority responds with knowing swagger ("I know what you two really want"), instantly advancing the plot.

7. **Commercial Button Arc (for Branded Stories):**
   - Allocate a 10s button: setup (2s) → slogan in authentic character voice with swagger inside `<d>[Language] ...</d>` (5s) → satisfying sensory crunch and hold (3s).
