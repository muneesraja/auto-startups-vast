# Naila - Episode 1: The Sanctuary's Joy

Target duration: 300 seconds (5 minutes)

## Narrative Breakdown

### Scene 1: Peaceful Morning & Unexpected Distress (75s)
In the serene heart of a sun-dappled rainforest clearing, Naila, a cheerful 6-year-old girl, naps peacefully on a rustic wooden swing tied to a towering banyan tree. Resting on the soft grass nearby is Azhagi, a loyal golden rescue dog with a fluffy tail, while Neju, a colorful green parrot, perches softly on a low branch above. Sunlight glints off the leafy canopy. Naila stirs and unexpectedly wakes up in distress, tears welling in her eyes as she starts crying softly. Azhagi lifts his head in concern, and Neju tilts his head attentively.

### Scene 2: Comfort Efforts & The Heroic Dash (75s)
Determined to comfort little Naila, Neju flutters down to the swing rope, making gentle chirping sounds, while Azhagi hops up and barks playfully, wagging his tail to cheer her up. However, Naila's crying intensifies. Recognizing that Naila needs her father, Azhagi turns alertly toward the forest trail, barks firmly toward Neju to guard the swing, and sprints at full speed down the winding dirt path through the shelter grounds.

### Scene 3: The Alarm & The Gallop to Rescue (75s)
Deeper in the shelter compound near spacious wooden enclosures, Naila's father stands beside a gentle Asian elephant, offering fresh sugarcane. Suddenly, Azhagi bursts out from the treeline, barking urgently and tugging gently at Father's khaki trousers. Realizing something is wrong at the swing area, Father immediately mounts his trusty brown rescue horse stationed nearby. With a swift nudge, the horse breaks into a fast gallop across the sanctuary grounds with Azhagi running alongside.

### Scene 4: Relief & High On Shoulders (75s)
As they reach the banyan tree, Father calls out Naila's name in a warm, reassuring voice. Hearing her father's voice, Naila wipes her eyes as her tears melt into a tender smile. Father dismounts smoothly and lifts Naila up high onto his broad shoulders. Naila opens her wide dark eyes, giggling as she gazes out across the vast, sunlit animal shelter—seeing the elephant, deer, Neju soaring overhead, and Azhagi wagging his tail happily below.
