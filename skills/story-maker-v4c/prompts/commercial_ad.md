# Commercial ad overlay

Read this **only** when the user brief is a commercial / product ad. Skip for
narrative films. No new parsed keys. Same MiniMax H3 syntax as the bible.

## One job per generation

A 15s H3 clip is not a full campaign. Split:

- ~8–15s hero → 1 generation
- 30s ad → 4–5 generations (problem, reveal, in-use, benefit, CTA hold)
- 60s explainer → ~6 beats across scenes/gens

Agent 1/2 put **one purpose** in each scene `beat`. Do not stuff problem +
three features + testimonial + price + CTA into one SHOT.

## S.C.E.N.E. + takeaway (every ad generation)

- **S**ubject — one product or person
- **C**ontext — place + who it serves
- **E**vent — what changes
- **N**uance — camera, light, sound, timing
- **E**xclusions — see below
- **Purpose** — what the viewer should understand by the end (durability,
  care, morning routine). Not “it looks cool.”

## Beat menu (pick one per generation)

Use [`coverage.md`](coverage.md) situations in parentheses.

- Product hero — centered pack, slow push-in, label lock (`discover` / `emotion`)
- Product in use — problem then natural use, MCU; no fake results
- Lifestyle — product clearly visible; supporting props only
- Packaging reveal — top-down, slow hands; avoid finger CU if distortion risk
- Launch teaser — details, then full reveal in the last seconds; leave space
  for overlay (do not paint fake launch text)
- Founder / documentary — observed workspace, not fake office polish

## Locks

On hero beats the product is the **sole visual owner**. Keep shape, color,
label, and pack unchanged. Real materials; no fake condensation or sparkle
particles. One slow push or short orbit. Ending = still, readable pack.

In-frame copy only if the user supplied the exact English line in `"quotes"`.
Otherwise leave empty space for overlay and ban generated text.

Never invent logos, claims, ratings, or testimonials.

## Exclusions (always)

Distorted hands / extra fingers, unreadable or misspelled labels, fake logos,
extra or duplicate products, fake testimonials / ratings / analytics, sudden
camera jumps, cluttered backgrounds.

Grounded wildlife still follows [`creature_behavior.md`](creature_behavior.md).
Cute mascot product-pets only if Tone is `stylized`.
